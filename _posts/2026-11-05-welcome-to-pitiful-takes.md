The podcast was not enough. Forty-five minutes of yelling about sports doesn't do this podcast justice, so now all the horrible takes are in writing.

## What goes here

- **Takes that didn't make the episode.** Somebody always gets cut off right before their best (worst) point.
- **Receipts.** There's nothing better than roasting your cohosts on awful takes.
- **Predictions.** Logged with a date, so nobody can get hindsight bias.

## The Pitiful-meter

Every post gets a score from 1 to 5 on how pitiful the take is:

1. **Actually Reasonable.** EXTREMELY rare. Can't miss these.
2. **Mildly Questionable.** You could argue it, if you know ball.
3. **Pretty Pitiful.** Most of what we say.
4. **Deeply Pitiful.** The host chat is going to have a field day with this.
5. **Career-Ending.** We'll be hearing about this one for years.

This post is a 1. It's all downhill from here.
