The podcast was not enough. Forty-five minutes of yelling about sports apparently doesn't cover it, so now we're writing things down too, which means there's a permanent record of every bad call we make.

## What goes here

- **Takes that didn't fit in the episode.** Somebody always gets cut off right before their best (worst) point.
- **Receipts.** When one of us is right, we're bringing it up forever. When one of us is wrong, everyone else is.
- **Predictions.** Logged with a date, so nobody can pretend later.

## The Pitiful-meter

Every post gets a score from 1 to 5 on how pitiful the take is:

1. **Actually Reasonable.** Rare. Treasure these.
2. **Mildly Questionable.** You could argue it, if you squint.
3. **Pretty Pitiful.** Most of what we say.
4. **Deeply Pitiful.** Group chat is already roasting it.
5. **Career-Ending.** We'll be hearing about this one for years.

This post is a 1. It's all downhill from here.
