Three rounds is a warm-up. Fighters are just figuring each other out and then the bell rings and it goes to the judges. Let's fix it.

## The proposal

- **Five rounds for everybody.** Main event, prelims, the guy making his debut at 2 p.m. Everyone goes 25 minutes.
- **No judges' decisions.** Still standing after five? The crowd votes by cheering.
- **Walkout songs get a time limit** so all of this still fits on one card.

## Why this works

Championship rounds are where legends get made. Rounds four and five are when the cardio runs out, the game plans go out the window and things get chaotic. More of that, please.
