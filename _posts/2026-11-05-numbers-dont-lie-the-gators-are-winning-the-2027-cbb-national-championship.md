Sure, it’s November, and picking my champion this early might bite me in the butt in April. But I don’t care. We’re winning it all, and here’s why.

## **Stat 1: History is literally repeating**

Florida opened the season No. 1 in the AP preseason poll, with 47 of 59 first-place votes. The last time the Gators started a season at No. 1 was 2006.

What happened that season? They won the 2007 national title and proceeded to go back-to-back under Billy Donovan.

## **Stat 2: Nobody left**

In the transfer portal era, teams lose their roster EVERY SPRING. Florida kept 10 players, including Thomas Haugh, Alex Condon, Rueben Chinyelu and Boogie Fland. Chinyelu even went through the NBA Combine and came back anyway.

Then Denzel Aberdeen, a piece of the 2025 title team, came home from Kentucky. And Florida is the only team in the country with three players on the NABC Player of the Year watch list. Three. Most teams would kill for one.

## **Stat 3: They were already this good**

The year after winning it all, Florida went 27-8, went 16-2 in the SEC and won the conference outright. They scored 87.1 points a game and went 14-1 at home.

All in a hangover season!

## **Stat 4: Opening night statement win**

Florida opened the season by beating No. 14 Miami 77-67 in Tampa. Thomas Haugh put up a double-double and hit four threes. That's four straight wins over the Canes, and the Gators now lead the all-time series 48-23.

The Pitiful Podcast Miami homers were silent on Monday.

## **But…**

**This almost identical Florida team lost to Iowa 73-72.**

Florida was a No. 1 seed. They had almost the same core. They had just won the SEC. Then they lost to No. 9 seed Iowa by one point in the second round. Before that game, the Gators were 20-2 all-time in the tournament against teams seeded ninth or lower.

So every stat above was also true last March. Great record, conference title, top seed, loaded frontcourt. None of it got them out of the first weekend. A national title takes six straight wins in March, and one bad night ends it. The numbers don't lie, but they also don't play the games.

One thing to consider is that Florida improved its weakness: guard play. After bringing back Denzel Aberdeen in the portal, they already look leagues better from behind the arc.

## **Pitiful Rating: 2. Mildly Questionable**

Not that pitiful. Florida is the best team in the country on paper, and I'd still take them over the field. Nevertheless, calling a national title in November after watching this team lose to a 9 seed eight months ago will raise some eyebrows.

Save this post. If they cut down the nets in April, I called it. If they lose in the second round again, I'll be very quiet this spring.

**GO GATORS**
