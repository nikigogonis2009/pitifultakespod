For years everyone has said running backs don't matter: don't pay them, don't draft them early, just plug in whoever and the scheme takes care of the rest.

I'm done pretending to agree.

## The case

A great back changes how a defense lines up on **every single snap**. Safeties creep down, linebackers stop dropping back, and suddenly your average quarterback has receivers running open downfield. That's not the scheme. That's the guy in the backfield scaring people.

> "You can't spread out a defense that's terrified of the run." — me, on the last episode, before getting talked over

## The counterargument (wrong)

"Running backs get hurt and fall off after 27." Sure. So do my knees, and I still use them every day.

## Final verdict

Pay the man. Draft the man. Give him the ball 25 times. I'll take my apology in the comments, whenever we figure out how to add comments.
