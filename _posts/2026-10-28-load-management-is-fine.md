Before you close the tab: I ran the numbers. They weren't conclusive, but I ran them.

The regular season is long and the stakes are low. A star who's healthy in May is worth more than one who played a random Tuesday in January and tweaked a hamstring doing it.

## "But I bought tickets"

You bought tickets to watch **basketball**. And you got basketball. Maybe from the eighth man in the rotation, but it was technically basketball.

## The stats

| Scenario | Games played | Playoff vibes |
|---|---|---|
| Plays all 82 | 82 | Exhausted |
| Sits 20 | 62 | Fresh legs, ring energy |
| Sits 40 | 42 | Okay that's too many |

I rest my case. Also my legs.
