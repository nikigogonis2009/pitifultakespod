The pitch clock made games faster and nobody misses the old version. So let's keep going.

## The proposal

- **Hard two-hour cap.** When the clock hits zero, the game ends.
- **Tied? Home run derby.** Three hitters per side, five swings each.
- **Mound visits** get replaced by one 15-second timeout per game.

## Why this works

Baseball fans love tradition, and nothing is more traditional than watching people hit dingers. This just gets us there faster.
