## The editor

Posts are written in **Pages CMS**, a free editor that saves straight to the website's GitHub repo. You get a normal writing screen with bold, italics, headings, links and image uploads, plus dropdowns for the author, sport and Pitiful-meter.

<p><a class="btn btn-red" href="https://app.pagescms.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the editor</a></p>

## First time only

1. Go to [app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org) and **sign in with GitHub**.
2. When it asks, **install the Pages CMS app** on the `pitifultakespod` repo.
3. Pick the `pitifultakespod` repo. You'll see **Blog posts** and **The Crew** in the sidebar.

Each crew member needs their own GitHub account, added as a collaborator on the repo (GitHub → repo **Settings → Collaborators**). Then they sign in the same way.

## Write a new post

1. Click **Blog posts**, then **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: the headline.
   - **Date**: today, or whenever it should be dated.
   - **Author**: you.
   - **Sport**: picks the color and the filter it shows up under. Use *Hot Take* for anything else.
   - **The take**: one sentence. It goes in the big red box at the top and in the scrolling bar on the home page.
   - **Pitiful-meter**: 1 is *Actually Reasonable*, 5 is *Career-Ending*.
   - **Post**: write the actual post.
3. Click **Save**. The site updates by itself in about a minute.

## Edit or delete a post

Open **Blog posts**, click the post, change what you want and **Save**. To delete it, use the menu next to the post in the list.

## Writing tips

- Use **headings** to break up a long post. They show up as big red-slashed titles.
- A **quote** becomes a big dark pull-quote. Save it for the line you want people to screenshot.
- Keep the take short. If it doesn't fit on one line of a phone, it's two takes.

## Edit the crew

**The Crew** in the editor holds everyone's name, nickname, jersey number, bio, photo and favorites. Changes show up on The Crew page and on the home page jerseys.
